# Section-by-Section Deep Guide

מדריך עומק לכל אחד מ-14 הסקשנים של ה-Master Template. השתמש בו כשאתה לא בטוח איזו מילה מקצועית להשתמש או איך להעמיק שדה.

---

## 🔹 INPUT CONTROL

המטרה: לתת ל-Seedance עוגן ויזואלי. אם יש start frame — לתאר אותו. אם לא — לתאר את הפריים הראשון של השוט.

**REFERENCE IMAGE / START FRAME** — תיאור פריים סטילס: הדמות במיקום, פוזה, מבט, סביבה. 2-4 משפטים.

**same character, identical facial structure, consistent proportions** — לציין מפורשות מה לא לשנות:
- "preserve exact face geometry from reference, identical jawline, ear positioning, eyebrow shape"
- "no morphing of identity, no aging, no facial restructuring across frames"
- "consistent body proportions: shoulder-to-hip ratio, hand size, neck length"

**stable lighting baseline** — נקודת התחלה לתאורה (טמפ' צבע + תיאור):
- "4200K mixed practical and ambient, soft directional from frame-left"
- "5600K daylight balanced with -1 stop fill from sky bounce"
- "2800K tungsten interior, hard shadows from single overhead source"

**consistent color baseline** — פלטת בסיס עם hex:
- "desaturated cool grays (#3A4751) base, warm amber accents (#C8843E) on skin"
- "monochromatic indigo (#1A2B5C) world, single red accent (#D63E2F) on subject"

**defined realism level**:
- "photorealistic 35mm film with subtle organic grain"
- "hyper-real digital, sensor-clean, micro-detail visible"
- "stylized cinematic, slight color separation pushed beyond natural"

## 🔹 CORE STYLE

**scene type** — תת-סוגה ספציפית:
- transformation reveal / chase / standoff / confession / first encounter / awakening / collapse / ritual / negotiation / surveillance / pursuit / discovery / loss / accusation / coronation

**realism level** — רמת ריאליזם פעם נוספת אבל יותר מפורט:
- "HBO prestige drama photorealism — every fabric weight physical, every dust particle obeys aerodynamics"
- "Denis Villeneuve large-format minimalism — sterile spaces, oppressive negative space"
- "Wes Anderson symmetrical stylization — flat compositions, hyper-saturated palette, theatrical staging"

**tone** — 3-5 שמות תואר/מילים רגשיות:
- "ominous awakening, sacred dread, inevitability, hushed reverence"

**world rules** — חוקי הפיזיקה/מציאות הייחודיים:
- "in this world, ceremonial attire physically materializes around the chosen sovereign through molecular reorganization of light into matter"
- "physics is normal, but emotional resonance distorts color — saturation increases with grief"
- "gravity bends 5° toward the antagonist's position when present in frame"

**character identity** — תיאור פיזי + נוכחות:
- "Mike, late 30s, athletic 1.85m frame, sharp jawline with 3-day stubble, eyes carry weight of recent loss; wears charcoal three-piece custom suit, no tie, top button open, polished oxford boots"

**physical state**:
- "fatigued from kilometers of pursuit, breath shallow, micro-tremor in fingers"
- "alert but composed, body coiled like a spring, weight on balls of feet"
- "wounded, favoring left side, breath catches on inhale"

**movement style**:
- "deliberate and heavy, every step intentional, weight emphasized through heel-strike"
- "feline economy — no wasted motion, glides between positions"
- "controlled urgency, military discipline restraining panic"

**intent** — מה הדמות רוצה ברגע הזה:
- "to confirm whether the throne recognizes him"
- "to reach the door before the corridor seals"
- "to delay the inevitable for three more seconds"

**transformation (if exists)**:
- "tailored suit dissolves at molecular level into segmented bronze armor plates"
- "fingers extend 30% in length as bone restructures"
- "human pupils elongate horizontally into goat-like rectangles"
- אם אין: `n/a — kinetic state remains constant throughout shot`

**trigger** — מה מתחיל את השינוי:
- "fingertips contact throne armrest"
- "eye contact with reflection"
- "third sub-bass tone of the activation chord"

**progression** — איך השינוי מתפרס בזמן:
- "0.4s: shimmer at fingertips → 1.2s: cascade up arms → 2.1s: chest plate solidifies → 3.0s: complete, helmet last"

**physical effect**:
- "spine straightens 3 inches taller, posture shifts from contained to imperial, gaze hardens"

## 🔹 MOTION LOGIC

**motion initiation** — מאיפה מתחילה תנועה:
- "from core (hips lead), shoulders follow, hands last"
- "from gaze (eyes shift first, head 4 frames later, body 8 frames after)"
- "from extremity (fingertip extends, kinetic chain reverses up arm)"

**kinetic chain** — איך זה מתפשט:
- "ground → calf → quad → hip → core → shoulder → arm → hand → fingertip"
- "head turn → spine spiral → hip rotation → leg cross-step"

**acceleration**:
- ease-in (slow start, builds)
- linear (constant speed)
- ease-out (fast start, slow finish)
- ease-in-out (S-curve)
- explosive (instant peak speed)
- elastic (overshoots and settles)

**timing** — שניות מדויקות לכל ביט:
- "0.0s: rest, 0.3s: anticipation crouch, 0.5s: launch, 1.2s: peak height, 2.0s: landing, 2.4s: settle"

**follow-through**:
- "after stopping, hair continues 0.4s, coat tail 0.6s, breathing settles over 1.5s"
- "punch follow-through: arm continues past target 30cm, reverses, returns to guard"

**overlap**:
- "while right arm extends, left arm pulls back simultaneously (counter-balance)"
- "footstep landing overlaps with previous foot's lift (running gait)"

**stopping logic**:
- "decelerates over 6 frames, micro-overshoot, settles with 2-frame oscillation"
- "hard stop with full muscle engagement, body absorbs momentum"

**effect integration** — איך VFX משתלב בתנועה:
- "transformation particles emerge from body in sync with breath rhythm, intensify on exhale"
- "energy effect builds with each footfall, peaks at moment of impact"

## 🔹 SEQUENCE STRUCTURE

חלוקה זמנית של 5-10 שניות לביטים:

**opening** (0-1s) — מה רואים ראשון:
- "frame opens on subject's silhouette against backlit doorway, no movement yet"

**build-up** (1-3s):
- "subject begins forward motion, camera pushes in, score introduces low drone"

**turning point** (השנייה המדויקת):
- "0:03.4 — subject's eyes lock onto throne, breath catches, micro-pause"

**escalation** (3-5s):
- "tempo doubles, camera matches subject's accelerating gait, score builds, color saturation rises"

**climax** (השיא):
- "0:05.8 — fingertip contacts throne, transformation initiates with sub-bass impact"

**payoff** (תשלום ויזואלי):
- "armor complete, subject straightens, stares directly into lens"

**resolution** (סגירה):
- "camera pulls back 1m revealing subject's full transformed silhouette against throne, last frame holds for 0.4s"

## 🎯 DIRECTOR LAYER

זה הכי חשוב. **תמלא את כל ה-10 שדות** עם מחשבה תכליתית.

**intent** — מה הצופה צריך להרגיש:
- "viewer should feel a chill of recognition — that the protagonist is becoming the very thing he feared"
- "viewer should feel oxygen-deprived urgency, want to look away but cannot"

**visual hierarchy** — סדר העין:
- "1st: subject's eyes (0:00-0:02), 2nd: throne in deep background (0:02-0:04), 3rd: transformation cascade across body (0:04-0:08)"

**moment of change** — הביט המדויק:
- "0:03.4 — the half-second where his expression shifts from defiance to acceptance, before the body knows"

**energy curve**:
- "starts at 3/10 (controlled), builds to 6/10 at midpoint, drops to 2/10 in micro-pause at 0:03.4, explodes to 9/10 at climax, settles at 7/10 hold"

**rhythm of action**:
- "two slow breaths, one held, then explosive — like a held cough finally released"

**spatial clarity** — איך ברור איפה הדברים:
- "high clarity — viewer always knows subject's position, throne's distance, exit's direction"
- "intentionally disorienting — spatial relationships unclear to mirror character's confusion"

**impact design** — איך נבנה הרגע המכה:
- "impact preceded by 0.3s silence, frozen frame, then sub-bass + visual chaos burst, then slow release"

**transformation philosophy** — המסר הסמלי:
- "transformation as imposed identity — Mike does not choose to become Emperor X, the ceremony chooses him; this is horror disguised as coronation"
- "metamorphosis as truth-revelation, not change — what he always was, finally visible"

**contrast** — ניגוד מכוון:
- "stillness against transformation — body remains motionless while material changes"
- "warmth of memory against coldness of becoming"
- "dignity of pose against violation of self"

**signature moment** — הפריים שיהיה פוסטר:
- "the moment armor reaches Mike's face but before helmet seals — half man, half emperor, eyes still human, looking directly into lens"
- "low-angle wide of fully-transformed silhouette, throne behind, light catching crown's edge"

## ⚙️ CONTROL VARIABLES

**motion intensity** (1-10):
- 1-3: meditative, slow, sparse motion
- 4-6: natural pacing, conversational
- 7-8: action-oriented, kinetic
- 9-10: extreme action, chaos

**camera stability**:
- locked: zero movement, tripod
- light handheld: 2-3% organic micro-shake
- handheld: visible breathing, 5-8% movement
- heavy handheld: chase-cam, 12-15% movement
- chaotic: documentary, fight-cam style

**realism priority**:
- photoreal-first (default for prestige)
- stylized-first (Wes Anderson, comic)
- hybrid (real lighting, stylized color)

**detail level**:
- standard: clean, expected detail
- high: textile weaves visible, individual hair strands
- ultra: sub-millimeter detail, pore-level skin
- extreme: every dust particle, every fiber, sensor-pushing
