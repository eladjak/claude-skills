## דוגמא 1: Transformation Shot (2097 / Emperor X)

**Brief מקורי**: "Mike walks toward the imperial throne. He places his hand on the armrest, and his tailored suit transforms into Emperor X's armor. He turns and looks at the camera."

```
=== SEEDANCE 2.0 MASTER PROMPT ===
// ASSUMPTIONS: Sci-fi prestige drama tone, derelict imperial setting, Mike is the established protagonist, this is the inciting transformation moment of the trailer.
// SHOT TITLE: Coronation Reveal — Mike Becomes Emperor X
// DURATION TARGET: 8 seconds

🔹 INPUT CONTROL
REFERENCE IMAGE / START FRAME: Mike, late 30s, athletic 1.85m frame in charcoal three-piece custom suit, no tie, top button open, stands 4 meters from a tarnished bronze imperial throne in a derelict atrium of vaulted marble. Shafts of pale blue light cut through a shattered stained-glass dome above. His back is half-turned to camera, hand frozen mid-reach toward the throne's armrest.
same character, identical facial structure, consistent proportions: preserve exact face geometry from reference, identical jawline angle (approximately 105°), ear positioning, eyebrow shape and arch, no morphing of identity across transformation, no aging, no facial restructuring; consistent body proportions throughout — shoulder-to-hip ratio 1.4:1, hand size, neck length unchanged.
stable lighting baseline: 4200K mixed — cold ambient daylight (5600K) bleeding through ceiling shatter, warm sodium practicals (2200K) from corridor behind throne, key:fill ratio approximately 4:1.
consistent color baseline: desaturated cool grays (#2A3F4F) for environment base, warm amber accents (#8B6F3D) on bronze throne and Mike's skin highlights, deep blacks crushed at #0A0E14.

🔹 CORE STYLE

🔹 SUBJECT + PERFORMANCE

transformation (if exists): tailored suit dissolves at molecular level into segmented bronze armor plates with chased filigree of obsidian inlay; armor follows body geometry exactly, no bulk added, edges catch light with mirror-bright specular highlights; helmet forms last, sliding up from collar plate to fully encase head except for eye slit.

🔹 CAMERA

movement: slow dolly-in from 4.5m to 2.0m subject distance over 8 seconds, motion ease-in-out, decelerates as it reaches final framing; 2% Steadicam-grade micro-stability for organic feel without visible shake.
depth of field / focus: shallow DOF f/2.0 throughout, focus locked on Mike's chest at start, ramps to face during last 2 seconds via subtle focus pull, no defocus moments — precision focus puller work; bokeh in background renders throne as soft amber-blue smear, dust motes visible as bright defocused circles.

🔹 ENVIRONMENT

🔹 LIGHTING
time of day: late afternoon, post-storm overcast, no direct sun — diffused daylight only.
light sources: key — 18K HMI through 12x12 silk diffusion at 5600K from camera-left at 30° elevation, simulating dome-broken daylight; fill — 4x4 white bounce camera-right at -2 stops; rim — 2K tungsten with full CTB at 5200K from above-behind at 60° elevation, +1 stop, defining shoulder line and head silhouette; practicals — 6 sodium lamps in deep background corridor at 2200K visible as warm bokeh points; eye light — small 600W LED panel below camera at -3 stops to catch corneal reflection; transformation light — internal "molecular" amber glow at 2700K emerging from suit fibers during transformation, building from -8 stops to 0 stops over 4 seconds.
direction: primary key 45° camera-left, slightly above eye-line, motivated by ceiling shatter; rim from above-behind motivated by similar source; transformation light radiates outward from body itself.

🔹 COLOR
color palette: cold cathedral blues (#1A2B3F base, #2A3F4F mid, #4F6A82 highlight) for environment, warm bronze and amber (#8B6F3D, #B8843E, #D89E5E) for throne and transformation, neutral skin tones in protected highlight, deep crushed blacks (#0A0E14).

color progression: shot opens neutral cool, transformation introduces warm amber that grows from a single fingertip point at 0:03.4 to fill 40% of frame by 0:07; final beat returns to balanced palette as armor settles, but amber glow lingers in eye slit highlight.

🔹 SOUND + MUSIC

transformation sound: low sub-bass swell building from -20dB to -3dB over 4 seconds (0:03.4 to 0:07.4), glass-shimmer cascade at 8-12kHz tracking the visual transformation flow, metallic stress crackle at each segment lock-in (12 distinct micro-impacts), deep impact at chest-plate completion (0:04.6) hitting -1dB sub-bass; helmet seal at 0:07.4 ends with 4-second decay sub-bass tail.
music tone: minimal Hans Zimmer-influenced — single sustained low cello drone at C2 throughout, brass swell entering at 0:03.4 (transformation start) building through climax, single piano note at C5 repeating every 4 beats from 0:05 onward, full orchestral hit at 0:04.6 (chest plate moment) then receding to drone for resolution.

🔹 PHYSICS
weight: subject body weight 80kg, movement reflects mass — gait emphasizes ground contact, no light-stepping; armor adds approximately 15kg, motion becomes 20% slower post-transformation, footfalls would be deeper if steps were taken.
inertia: movement decelerates over 4-6 frames, no instant stops; head turn at end (0:07.6) accelerates over 8 frames, decelerates over 6 frames, no overshoot.
force transfer: footsteps before transformation transfer through marble with audible bass; transformation pressure wave at 0:03.4 transfers outward radially, displacing dust within 2m and creating visible air ripple.
environmental reaction: each early step compresses dust into 30cm plume, plume falls back over 1.2s; transformation pulse repels ambient dust outward in 360° wave; banner remnants frame-left flutter in response to pressure wave.
resistance: moderate air resistance visible on suit coat trailing behind walking motion (0.4s lag); during transformation, no air resistance applies to the materializing armor — it manifests in place.
recoil: at chest-plate completion (0:04.6), Mike's body absorbs the formation impact through micro-flex of knees (2cm dip and recovery over 6 frames); n/a for projectile recoil.
gravity: Earth standard 9.8m/s² throughout, dust falls at expected rate; no gravity manipulation during transformation despite mystical context.
secondary motion (hair / clothes): short hair has minimal lag (2-frame settle), suit coat trails 0.4s behind body motion before transformation; during transformation, fabric does not flutter normally — it crystallizes from outside-in, edges first; after armor completes, minimal secondary motion as plates are rigid; only soft cloth element remaining is interior padding visible at neck collar gap.

🔹 MOTION LOGIC
motion initiation: pre-transformation, Mike's motion initiates from gaze (eyes shift to throne first, head 4 frames later, body 8 frames after); transformation initiates from fingertip contact point and propagates outward through kinetic chain in reverse (extremity to core to extremity).
kinetic chain: hand → forearm → bicep → shoulder → chest → core → opposite shoulder → opposite arm; then descends from core → hips → thighs → calves → feet; helmet completes from neck → skull → crown.
acceleration: pre-transformation walking is linear, controlled; transformation cascade is ease-in-out per body segment but overall arc accelerates from 0:03.4 to peak velocity at 0:05.4 then decelerates; final head turn at 0:07.6 is ease-in with held finish.
timing: 0:00.0 — rest, frozen reach. 0:00.4 — micro-step forward. 0:01.6 — full step landing. 0:02.4 — second step. 0:03.0 — hand reaches forward. 0:03.4 — contact and ignition. 0:04.6 — chest plate climax. 0:06.2 — leg armor complete. 0:07.4 — helmet seal. 0:07.6 — head turn initiates. 0:08.0 — eye contact with lens, frame freezes for final hold.
follow-through: after each pre-transformation footfall, body settles over 4 frames; after chest plate impact, micro-vibration through marble floor visible for 8 frames; after helmet seal, no follow-through (rigid).
overlap: while right hand reaches forward, left hand pulls back slightly for balance (counter-balance overlap); during transformation cascade, multiple body regions transform simultaneously with staggered timing (chest peaking while arms still in mid-cascade).
stopping logic: pre-transformation walks decelerate over 6 frames with micro-overshoot then settle; transformation cascade per-segment hard-stops with 2-frame lock animation (segments snapping into final position with definitive timing).
effect integration: transformation amber light tracks ahead of physical material change by 2 frames (light arrives, then matter follows); dust repulsion synchronized with chest-plate hit; sound design hits exactly with visual segment locks.

🔹 SEQUENCE STRUCTURE
opening: 0:00-0:01 — frame opens on Mike standing 4m from throne, hand extended mid-reach, frozen in moment of decision; cold blue light cascading through dome, dust drifting; viewer registers location and stakes.

escalation: 0:03.4-0:05.4 — transformation cascades outward and upward, brass enters score, color saturation rises 20%, camera continues push-in slightly accelerated, dust repelled in radial wave.

payoff: 0:05.4-0:07.4 — armor completes outward through limbs and downward through legs, helmet forms last, score recedes to drone; visual payoff is the imperial silhouette resolving from Mike's frame.
resolution: 0:07.6-0:08.0 — fully-formed Mike-as-Emperor turns head 90° toward lens, eye slit reveals still-human eyes locked onto camera, micro-pause holds for final 0.4s; the man inside the armor is what the viewer cannot forget.

🔹 EDITING STYLE
shot structure: single take, no cuts, 8-second sustained shot (oner). All transformation occurs in real-time on-screen.
cut timing: no internal cuts.
rhythm: respiratory rhythm — slow deliberate pacing in build-up matching breath, sub-bass surge as held-breath release at 0:03.4, sustained tension through climax, sigh-decay in resolution.

spatial continuity: throne always frame-right deep ground, columns flanking left and right symmetrically receding, dome above unchanged across shot.

🎯 DIRECTOR LAYER

visual hierarchy: 1st (0:00-0:01) — Mike's silhouette and reaching hand; 2nd (0:01-0:03) — the throne emerging from background as camera pushes in; 3rd (0:03-0:07) — the transformation cascade across body; 4th (0:07-0:08) — the human eyes inside the imperial helmet.
```

```
// SHOT TITLE: Slide-and-Return — Wet Alley Engagement

same character, identical facial structure, consistent proportions: face obscured by tactical mask (only eyes visible), preserve eye spacing, eyebrow arch, identical body proportions, weapon grip identical across frames, mask design unchanged.
stable lighting baseline: 5400K cool ambient with warm 2400K sodium street lamp practicals and saturated 4800K cool magenta neon signage, key:fill ratio 6:1.
consistent color baseline: deep blacks (#0A0A0E), wet asphalt slate (#2A2E36) reflecting magenta (#C8326E) and amber (#D88830) practicals, muzzle flash white-orange (#FFF0D0).
defined realism level: gritty urban photorealism, tactile water and fabric weight, hyper-real digital sensor look (RED V-Raptor 8K).

🔹 PHYSICS
```
